How the dosing works

The axle is the only part of the drum that doesn't move. Feed through it.

A fixed tube runs inside your hollow axle from a gland on the left post. Cross holes near the middle of the drum drip the dose into the bath. The bath stays under half full, so the axle and the holes stay above the liquid line.

Assumptions and what this model doesn't do
  • The frame is redrawn from your sketch. Post spacing and axle height are scaled from it; the bucket's 140 mm bottom radius, 15 gal size and 5 gal max water load are from your earlier model. Everything else is assumed.
  • The pH response is a toy model meant to show the control loop. Real response depends on your recipe, hide weight, float and chemical strength. The targets are example values; use your recipe's numbers.
  • Process time is compressed about 20×. Drum rotation is shown at the selected speed.
  • Tubing is drawn translucent so you can see the flow. Norprene is opaque black.
  • No structural, seal, torque or electrical sizing. Part names are candidates, not verified fits.
